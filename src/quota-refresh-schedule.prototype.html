How often should Pi Quota ask again?

Drive a proposed refresh schedule through work, idle time, provider resets, and failures. Every elapsed minute redraws the footer. Some minutes also make a real request to the active provider.

Question being tested. Does frequent polling during model work, followed by 1, 2, 5, and 15 minute idle intervals, keep the active provider's quota current without continuing one request per minute forever?
While workingRequest once per minute. Starting work requests immediately.
After workRequest when work settles, then after 1, 2, 5, and 15 minutes. Stay at 15 minutes.
Known resetRequest at the provider's reset time, even when the idle interval would wait longer.
FailureWait at least 2, 5, then 15 minutes after consecutive failures. A success clears failure backoff.

What to judge

Try the long-idle and failure walkthroughs first. The policy deliberately reaches a 15-minute ceiling rather than stopping. The key question is whether that ceiling is still too noisy, or whether a footer claiming current quota should never go longer than 15 minutes without real provider data.
